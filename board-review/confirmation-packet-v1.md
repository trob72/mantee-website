# Town of Mantee Website Confirmation Packet v1

## Purpose

This packet is for board/staff review before moving from planning and prototype work into WordPress staging.

The goal is to confirm the Town's official facts, launch scope, content ownership, and implementation decisions without inventing fees, rules, contacts, legal language, or public procedures.

## Recommended Direction for Approval

- Build a low-cost official Town of Mantee public information website.
- Use managed WordPress for the production site.
- Prefer a native WordPress block theme and block editor.
- Pursue a `.gov` domain through the authorized government request process.
- Treat the website as the official online source.
- Use Facebook to distribute links back to website pages.
- Launch current-use information first.
- Backfill historical records after launch in a prioritized archive queue.
- Keep the plugin stack small and justified.
- Keep all public information accessible, mobile-friendly, and easy for staff to maintain.

## Launch Scope Confirmation

Recommended first launch:

- Home page.
- Public Notices.
- Meetings.
- Ordinances currently in force: NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.
- Building Permits.
- Resident Services.
- Community Center.
- Community page with Farmers Market, Walking Track, Mantee Depot, Mantee History & Heritage Museum, and Events.
- Contact.
- Current approved documents and forms.

Recommended after launch:

- Historical meeting records.
- Historical public notices.
- Historical ordinances.
- Older scanned records.
- Expanded searchable document archives if needed.
- Community Center reservation request workflow if approved.
- Newsletter, alerts, online payments, or permit intake only after policy/workflow review.

## Approval Questions

1. Should the project proceed with a managed WordPress production site?
2. Should the Town pursue a `.gov` domain now?
3. Should the first launch focus on current-use public information while older records are backfilled later?
4. Who is authorized to approve final website content?
5. Who will own the domain, hosting, WordPress admin, and backup recovery access?
6. Which records should be included in the first launch?
7. Which information must remain offline until reviewed further?
