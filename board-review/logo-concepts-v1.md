## Included Concepts

### Concept A: Text-First Official

Simple `M` monogram with a strong Town of Mantee wordmark.

Recommended if the first website launch should avoid a formal seal approval process.

### Concept B: Water Tower Landmark

Simplified water tower mark paired with the Town wordmark.

Recommended if the Town wants a distinctly local identity that matches the current hero photography.

### Concept C: Trace + Crossroads

Road, horizon, and crossroads-inspired direction.

Potentially strong, but the Trace wording and historical claim require confirmation before public use.

### Concept D: Depot + Heritage

Depot-inspired mark.

Likely better as a community/history section accent than the primary Town mark.

### Concept E: Formal Seal Direction

Traditional circular seal direction.

This should be treated as a later official identity project, not a quick website-launch choice.

## Practical Recommendation

Narrow board discussion to two launch-safe choices:

- Concept A if clarity and low approval risk matter most.
- Concept B if local identity matters most.

Keep the current website header temporary until the board or staff selects a direction.
