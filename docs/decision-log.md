# Decision Log

This log tracks project decisions for the Town of Mantee website. It should be updated whenever the Town or authorized project owner confirms a direction.

Unknown or unapproved items must remain marked `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`.

## How to Use This Log

- Add the decision date.
- Identify who approved or requested the decision.
- Record the decision in plain language.
- Link to supporting notes, documents, or meeting records if available.
- Record any follow-up task.
- Do not treat recommendations as approved decisions until confirmed.

## Decision Status Labels

- `PROPOSED` - Recommended but not yet approved.
- `APPROVED` - Confirmed by the Town or authorized project owner.
- `DEFERRED` - Deliberately postponed.
- `REJECTED` - Considered and declined.
- `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` - Not ready for decision or missing facts.
