# Regional Reference Site Review

This document records nearby municipal website references for the Town of Mantee project. The goal is not to copy another town's site, but to identify patterns that fit a small Mississippi municipality with limited maintenance capacity.

## Sites Reviewed

| Site | URL | Working Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City of Eupora | https://cityofeuporams.gov/ | Best overall reference for Mantee's balance of civic information, local identity, and resident resources. |
| Town of Mathiston | https://www.mathiston.ms.gov/ | Strong official `.gov` credibility and simple service links, but visually generic. |
| Houston, Mississippi | https://www.houston.ms.gov/ | Similar to Mathiston; useful for basic government/service organization. |
| City of Starkville | https://www.cityofstarkville.org/ | Useful as a cautionary reference; strong service depth, but too large-city and portal-heavy for Mantee. |

## Overall Recommendation

Use Eupora as the closest high-level model, then simplify it for Mantee.

The best direction is:

- Eupora-inspired homepage balance.
- Mathiston/Houston-level official clarity.
- Mantee-specific photography and community identity.
- Less visual clutter than Starkville.
- Fewer widgets and third-party embeds than Eupora.

Working phrase:

**Eupora-inspired, Mantee-scaled.**

## Eupora Reference

### What Works

- Homepage feels more complete and community-facing than the state-template sites.
- Navigation separates government, departments, residents, and things to do.
- Public Notices and City Ordinances are visible from the homepage.
- Events and community resources are not buried.
- Contact information is easy to find.
- It feels more active and local than a plain government template.

### What To Borrow

- Homepage section rhythm: welcome, timely items, essential resources, notices/documents, contact.
- Dedicated space for public notices and ordinances.
- Dedicated community/resource cards.
- Simple plain-language labels.
- `.gov` domain credibility.

### What To Avoid

- Repeated headings and duplicated card labels.
- Too many embedded services or external widgets on the homepage.
- Account/sign-in features that are not needed for Mantee.
- Overloaded navigation for a small town.
- Publishing unconfirmed forms, hours, notices, or event information.

## Mathiston and Houston References

These two appear to use a similar Mississippi government template pattern.

### What Works

- Strong official presentation through the `.gov` domain and Mississippi government framework.
- Simple navigation.
- Practical quick links for water payments, services, permits, contact, news, and forms.
- Familiar government layout.
- Good model for low-maintenance information architecture.

### What To Borrow

- Official top-strip concept.
- Clear service-first calls to action.
- Practical department/service grouping.
- Simple footer with official contact and state links where appropriate.

### What To Avoid

- Generic visual identity.
- Text-heavy homepage hero.
- Too little community photography or place identity.
- Making Mantee feel like a copied state template.

## Starkville Reference

### What Works

- Strong quick-service orientation.
- Clear access to agendas, minutes, ordinances, maps, jobs, alerts, and reporting.
- Robust for a larger city with many departments.

### Why It Is Not The Right Fit

- Too busy and portal-like for Mantee.
- More corporate/civic-platform than small-town official hub.
- The visual tone does not match the rural heritage/community direction.
- It would suggest more staffing and workflow complexity than Mantee likely wants to maintain.

## Mantee Prototype Implications

### Navigation

Keep the current structure, but consider grouping future WordPress navigation around:

- Government
- Public Records
- Resident Services
- Community
- Contact

For the static prototype, keep the existing page links visible and simple.

### Homepage

Recommended homepage pattern:

1. Strong local hero with the Mantee water tower.
2. Official status strip.
3. Latest public information.
4. Resident quick links.
5. Community places and identity.
6. Records/documents area.
7. Contact and distribution note.

This borrows Eupora's active-community structure without carrying over its clutter.

### Visual Style

Preserve the current direction:

- Deep navy for official framing.
- Muted green for primary actions.
- Cream/off-white for warmth.
- Local aerial photography for identity.
- Clean cards and buttons.
- Minimal decorative effects.

Future refinements can make the site feel slightly more like Eupora's blend of function and community, but the prototype should remain calmer and more official.

### Content Strategy

The reference sites confirm that Mantee should not rely on Facebook as the official archive. Facebook can distribute announcements, but the website should hold:

- Public notices.
- Meeting agendas and minutes.
- Ordinances.
- Building permit information.
- Resident service information.
- Community Center information.
- Community and event pages.
- Contact information.
