## Archive Strategy

Recommended launch model:

- Launch current-use public information first.
- Backfill historical records after launch in a prioritized archive queue.
- Use a public page or record entry plus a linked file for high-value records.
- Keep archive gaps visible internally until resolved.

Historical archive scope and public wording are NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.

## Navigation Notes

- Keep top navigation short.
- Use homepage quick links for high-priority tasks.
- Link Facebook posts back to website URLs.
- Do not bury records under broad labels such as "Resources" when a direct label is clearer.
