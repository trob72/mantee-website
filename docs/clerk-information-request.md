# Clerk Information Request

This document is a shareable request list for the Town Clerk or other Town information keeper.

It is intentionally limited to factual information, source documents, and existing Town workflows. It does not ask the clerk to make website design, layout, branding, hosting, domain, plugin, navigation, or launch strategy decisions.

## Suggested Message

Hi Courtney,

We are gathering verified Town information for the future official Town of Mantee website. At this stage, we are not asking you to make website design or technology decisions. We are only trying to collect accurate Town facts, approved documents, and existing workflows so placeholders on the draft site can eventually be replaced with verified information.

If an item is unknown, not currently approved, or needs board/legal review, please mark it that way rather than guessing. Existing documents, forms, agendas, minutes, ordinances, flyers, or written procedures are especially helpful.

Thank you.

## What Courtney Does Not Need To Decide

- Website colors, design, layout, or style.
- Logo or official mark direction.
- Homepage button order or navigation choices.
- GitHub, WordPress, hosting, plugins, or technical setup.
- Whether to pursue a `.gov` domain.
- Final launch approval.
- New policies, new fees, or new rules.
- Legal interpretation of ordinances.

## Response Template

Courtney can use this structure for each item:

```text
Area:
Confirmed information:
Source document or source person:
Approved for public website use? yes/no/unknown:
Needs board/legal review? yes/no/unknown:
Notes:
```

When a response is received, process it internally with `docs/content-intake-template.md` and track it in `docs/content-source-log.csv` before changing prototype pages.

## Good Answers

These are all useful responses:

- "Confirmed: office hours are [provided by Courtney]."
- "Unknown: I need to check with the board."
- "We have a form, but I do not know if it is approved for the website."
- "This should not be published until legal/board review."
- "This is handled by the county, not the Town."
- "I can provide the document, but the wording should not be summarized."

## Avoid

- Guessing phone numbers, emails, fees, rules, or procedures.
- Creating new policy wording just for the website.
- Interpreting ordinances.
- Approving photos without confirming permission.
- Deciding design, layout, hosting, plugins, or launch timing.
