# Content Confirmation Checklist

This checklist turns the current prototype placeholders into a practical fact-gathering plan.

Use it before replacing `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` text in the prototype or before building the managed WordPress site. The goal is to confirm official facts without inventing fees, rules, contacts, names, ordinance language, or procedures.

Related source documents:

- `docs/content-inventory.csv`
- `docs/needs-confirmation-register.csv`
- `docs/clerk-information-request.md`
- `board-review/confirmation-packet-v1.md`
- `docs/decision-log.md`

## Confirmation Rules

- Keep `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` visible until a fact is confirmed by the Town or an authorized reviewer.
- Do not publish final phone numbers, emails, names, fees, permit requirements, rental rules, or legal language from memory.
- If a page is not ready for confirmed public use, either leave it out of launch navigation or keep the content explicitly marked as draft during review.
- Facebook can distribute links, but the website should remain the official source once launched.
- Draft photos require approval for public website use before final launch.

## Launch Blockers

These items should be confirmed before the site is treated as the official public website.

- [ ] Confirm who has authority to approve final website content.
- [ ] Confirm the website role: official source, with Facebook used for distribution.
- [ ] Confirm official Town contact details: physical address, mailing address, phone, email, and office hours.
- [ ] Confirm public records request routing.
- [ ] Confirm meeting schedule, meeting location, agenda workflow, and minutes workflow.
- [ ] Confirm ordinance publication source, current ordinance list, codification status, and disclaimer language.
- [ ] Confirm building permit process, required forms, fees, inspection/review contacts, and wording.
- [ ] Confirm resident service list and reporting/routing workflows.
- [ ] Confirm Community Center facility details, rental process, rules, fees, deposits, and contact.
- [ ] Confirm which photos are approved for public use.
- [ ] Confirm hosting, domain, administrator ownership, and backup administrator.
- [ ] Confirm final launch approval path.
