Header and Logo Direction Concepts

These are non-final visual directions for discussion only. They do not represent an approved Town seal, logo, or website header.

Review question Which direction feels most appropriate for Mantee: official civic, heritage, landmark, or text-first?

How to use this page: react to tone and fit, not exact artwork. The final mark, wording, and header behavior should be approved separately before launch.

Concept A

Official Civic

Safest municipal feel: simple seal-style mark, formal wordmark, strong official source signal.

M 1904
Town of Mantee Official Town Website
Notices Meetings Services Contact
  • Best if the board wants the most traditional official look.
  • Works well before a permanent seal or illustrated logo exists.
  • Easy to reproduce in WordPress and documents.
Concept B

Depot and Heritage

Leans into local history, railroad heritage, the museum, and the downtown community story.

Town of Mantee Heritage, Records, and Services
Government Services Community Records
  • Best if the board wants community identity to show up immediately.
  • Pairs naturally with Depot and Heritage Museum content.
  • Needs care so the official-government function still feels primary.
Concept C

Water Tower Landmark

Uses a recognizable local landmark and pairs well with the current aerial photo direction.

Town of Mantee Mississippi
Public Notices Meetings Resident Services
  • Best if the board wants something distinctly local but still official.
  • Strong match for the drone/water tower photography.
  • Could become a simple one-color mark for forms and social graphics.
Concept D

Text-First Official

Keeps branding restrained until the Town is ready to approve a permanent logo or seal.

M
Town of Mantee Official Town Website
Notices Meetings Permits Contact
  • Best if the board wants the least risky launch identity.
  • Can later accept an official seal without redesigning the site.
  • Very maintainable in managed WordPress.

Header Behavior Concept

Sticky Translucent Header

This is a static mockup of the effect: the header stays in view over photography and uses a translucent dark civic tone. If selected, it should still meet contrast and mobile usability requirements.

Town of Mantee Official Town Website
Home Government Services Community Contact

Official Information

Town of Mantee

Public notices, meeting records, resident services, and community information in one official place.

Why It Could Work

It makes the site feel more polished and keeps core navigation visible without covering the page in a heavy banner.

Watchouts

It needs strong contrast over photos, a compact mobile version, and simple dropdown behavior if menus expand later.

Suggested Board Feedback

  • Which direction feels most appropriate for Mantee?
  • Should the first version stay text-first, or should it include a landmark-inspired mark?
  • Does the translucent sticky header feel professional enough for an official Town website?
  • Are there any symbols, wording, or local references that should not be used?
