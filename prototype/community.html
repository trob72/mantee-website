Mantee Marketplace

The Mantee Marketplace is available sunup to sundown every day for farmers, gardeners, and cottage food makers to use the market space to sell produce or cottage food items.

There is no rental fee at this time. Vendors are responsible for keeping the area clean, returning tables to the back wall, and following applicable municipal, state, and federal rules.

Open flyer

Walking Track

The Walking Track is available from sunup to sundown. Contact the Town with maintenance questions or concerns.
