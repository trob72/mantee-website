  • Notice

    Blight prevention notice

    Publish dates: October 24 and October 31, 2025

    On October 6, 2025, the Board of Alderman of Mantee, Mississippi, passed Ordinance #2501, Blight Ordinance. This ordinance replaces the 2016 ordinance of the same topic to prevent, reduce, or eliminate blight or causes of blight within the Town of Mantee by the regulation of uncompleted, abandoned, dilapidated, or deteriorated buildings; to provide for the removal of building material of any kind whatsoever from land in the city limits; and to provide penalties for the violation of the ordinance. This ordinance will take effect 30 days after ratification.

    Copies of the full text are available at the Mantee Depot (Town Hall) and Mantee Community Center, or can be provided upon request at townofmantee@gmail.com.

    Publish: 10/24, 10/31/2025

  • Notice

    Mobile food vending notice

    Publish date: December 8, 2023

    On December 4, 2023, the Board of Alderman of Mantee, Mississippi, passed Ordinance #2401, Mobile Food Vending Ordinance. This ordinance provides guidelines for the operation of mobile food trucks within the town limits of Mantee, Mississippi, throughout the year. In summary, food trucks will be allowed to operate in the parking lot north of the Mantee Depot or on private property, with written permission from the property owner, between the hours of 6:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. any day of the year, except during town-appointed special events, and will be required to submit the specified paperwork and a $25 annual fee.

    Copies of the full text are available at the Mantee Depot (Town Hall), Mantee Community Center, and Webster County Courthouse or can be provided upon request. Interested food truck operators should contact the Town of Mantee at townofmantee@gmail.com for the application and more information.

    Publish: 12/8/2023

  • Notice

    Dwelling ordinance notice

    Publish dates: March 13 and March 20, 2026

    On March 2, 2026, the Board of Alderman of Mantee, Mississippi, passed Ordinance #2601, Dwelling Ordinance.

    This ordinance replaces the 2011 ordinance of the same topic to clarify minimum standards for dwellings within the Town of Mantee limits in order to protect public health, safety, welfare, and property values and to promote orderly development.

    Copies of the full text are available at the Mantee Depot (Town Hall) and Mantee Community Center, or can be provided upon request at townofmantee@gmail.com.

    Publish: 3/13, 3/20/2026
