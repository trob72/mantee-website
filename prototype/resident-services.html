Resident service information is organized by what is handled by the Town versus another agency.

Resident Services

Resident Services

Information for building permits, business privilege licenses, transient vendor licenses, and common resident concerns.

Town-Handled Services

  • Building permit information and application routing.
  • Privilege license applications for permanent businesses.
  • Transient vendor privilege license applications.
  • General concern routing through the official Town contact process.

Service Boundary

Most other services are handled by county, state, utility, emergency, or outside agencies. Contact the Town for routing help when the correct agency is unclear.

Town Services

Building permits are handled directly by the Town. Privilege license applications are available for businesses and transient vendors.

Report a Concern

Residents may contact the Town by email or through the Town Facebook page for common concerns.

County and State Links

County, state, utility, and emergency services are generally handled outside Town government. Contact the appropriate agency directly when known.

Privilege Licenses

Form

New businesses complete the applicable form and submit it to the Town Clerk. Existing businesses receive a reminder notice by mail but remain responsible for paying the yearly fee.

Transient vendor applicants should submit the transient vendor application and required supporting documents to the Town Clerk.
