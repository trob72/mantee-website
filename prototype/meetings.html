Board of Alderman

Board of Alderman Meetings

Regular Town board meetings are held monthly. Meetings are open to the public, and meeting records are available upon request via email.

Regular Meeting Schedule

  • Regular meetings are held monthly on the first Monday at 7:00 p.m.
  • Meeting location: Town Hall, 67 First Street, Mantee, MS 39751.
  • Meetings are open to the public.
  • Meeting minutes are approved at the following month's meeting.
  • Special meeting notices are posted in three public locations when applicable.

Meeting Records

Meeting records are available upon request by emailing the Town at townofmantee@gmail.com.

Special Meeting Notices

Special meeting notices are posted separately when applicable.

  • Meeting

    Regular monthly board meeting

    First Monday of each month at 7:00 p.m.

    Location: Town Hall, 67 First Street.

    Special meeting notices are posted in three public locations: on the door of the Depot/Town Hall, in the Post Office, and in the window of the Community Center.
