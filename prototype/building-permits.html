Resident Services

Building Permits

The Town handles building permits for new residential dwellings and commercial buildings within Town limits.

Download application

Permit Scope

  • The permit application was approved by the Mantee Board of Aldermen on March 2, 2026.
  • The application is for each new residential dwelling and commercial building planned within Town limits.
  • The application states it is not for outdoor buildings not intended for residential dwelling or commercial use.
  • The application states it is not for additions or renovations to existing residential dwellings and buildings.

Permit Questions

Contact the Town before beginning work for permit requirements, supporting documents, and next steps.

Before You Start

Contact the Town before beginning new construction to review permit requirements and make sure all supporting documents are ready.

Required Supporting Documents

  • Copy of valid deed or proof of ownership of the property.
  • Proof of adherence to applicable water and sewer/septic regulations.
  • Sketch of the lot and proposed building.
  • Proof of Mississippi 811 locate request.

Forms and Fees

Form

Application fees listed on the form are $10 for residential permits and $20 for commercial permits. Applications are not processed until the form is complete and all required information and payment are received.

Permit Contact

Use the official Town contact route for permit questions.
